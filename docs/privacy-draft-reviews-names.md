## The Roam Access pilot app: your reviews, your name and your access needs

### What others can see

Reviews in the Roam Access app are **public**. Anyone can read them, including on the web,
without signing in. Each review shows:

- **The name you choose** for your reviews. We suggest your first name and last initial (for
  example "Sam K."), and a nickname is fine. You can choose **Post anonymously**, and your
  reviews then show "Roam Access member".
- **How you describe yourself**, if you choose to say: "Lived experience" (you have a disability
  or access need), "Carer" (a carer, family member or support worker), or "Ally" (you want to
  help). This is self-described. We don't check it. You can also choose "Prefer not to say".
- **Your access needs** (for example "Wheelchair user"), **only if you turn this on**. It's off
  until you choose it. It applies to all your reviews, and you can turn it off at any time.
  When it's off, your access needs aren't shown with your reviews, photos, the entrances you
  mark or the arrival steps you share.
- The place, your answers, your words, your photos and the date.

If you show a name, you also get a **public profile page**. It shows your name, how you
describe yourself, your access needs (only if you turned that on), when you joined, your
contributor badges, how many reviews you've written, how many people your reviews helped, and
your reviews. People who post anonymously don't have a profile page. Profile pages use a random
link, never your email or your account ID, and we ask search engines not to list them.

"Top contributors this month" in the app lists people who show a name, with the number of
reviews they wrote in the last 30 days. It never shows access needs.

### Reviews from before names were shown

Reviews written before 8 November 2026 were written on the promise that they'd show only your
lived experience, never your name. They keep showing it that way until you choose a name or
change your settings yourself. If you do, all your reviews, including the older ones, show what
you chose.

### Access needs are sensitive information

Your access needs are health information, which the Privacy Act treats as sensitive. We use
them to personalise what the app shows you. We show them on your reviews only with your
explicit consent (the setting above). We never sell them, and we never share them with venues,
councils or advertisers. We don't record them in logs.

### "This helped me" and badges

When you tap "This helped me" on a review, we store that you did, so that you can only do it
once per review. The writer sees how many people their reviews helped, but not who. Contributor
badges (like "First review" or "Entrance finder") are worked out from what you've already
contributed. We don't collect anything extra for them.

### If a name breaks the rules

If a name is offensive, the pilot team can remove it. Your reviews then show "Roam Access
member" until you choose a new name.

### Changing your mind, and deleting your account

You can change your name, your description, the anonymous setting and whether your access
needs show at any time, in **Me → How I appear on reviews**. **Me → Delete my account** deletes
your profile, your access needs, your reviews, your photos, your "This helped me" taps and
everything else linked to your account. This can't be undone.
