Roam Access — simpler mobile app design mockups

Clickable HTML mockups of the redesigned phone app, from the spec. They use the app's palette and meet WCAG 2.2 AA on their own: every control is a real button or link, targets are 48 px or more, verdicts are icon + colour + words, and the text size buttons at the top of every screen show 150 % and 200 %.

Open them on a phone, or in a desktop browser at about 390 px wide. Nothing here talks to a server; the data is made up.

Screens

  1. Set up my needsFirst run: needs as big checkboxes with icons, Skip, then how I appear on reviews.
  2. Find (home)One search box with a mic, Near me, four big categories, and the list straight away. No map.
  3. Results (list first, map optional)Two filters, a plain summary line, List/Map switch. The map is a view of the same list, with 48 px pins and no drag-only sheet.
  4. PlaceVerdict, why, actions, today's problems, reviews — then everything else folded under "More about this place". Report a problem is a one-screen dialog.
  5. Getting thereWhich door, directions to it in one tap, maps app choice, parking and transport, steps from the street.
  6. Review (lived experience or carer)Three short steps: how was it · two questions for your needs · anything else. Send from step 1.
  7. Review as an ally"What did you notice?" with no verdict. Shown as "Observed by an ally".
  8. SavedYour places, with updates from them at the top and a count on the tab.
  9. MeNeeds, how I appear, badges, help out, settings, account. One list, no cards inside cards.

How to read them

  • Every screen has one main action. It is the big teal button.
  • Tabs: Find · Saved · Me. The map is never a tab.
  • Press Tab to see the focus order; the ring is 3 px blue on every control.
