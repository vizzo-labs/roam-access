Step by step from the street

From a wheelchair user, 2 November 2026.

  1. From the ferry, turn left along the Esplanade for about 50 metres.
  2. Turn left into Wharf Lane. It slopes gently down.
  3. Go through the car park. The ramp and the door are at the far right corner.
